Power → Damage Brackets

Final damage = your Power ÷ the stage's recommended Power, snapped to these 8 brackets. Below 10% you deal just 1% damage — an under-power team literally can't scratch the boss, which is why raising Power (Resolve, promotions, runes) breaks walls faster than swapping cookies. At 120% you get a 20% damage bonus.

Breaking Progression Walls

When your meta team stalls on an X-30 boss, it's account power, not comp. These are the levers.

Diagnose first: Team wipes before the boss dies → survival problem (DEF/HP/tank/heals). Timer expires with team alive → damage problem (ATK / DEF-down / uptime).
